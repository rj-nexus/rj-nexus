# Changelog

## [v1.12.0] - 2026-11-26

This release introduces the **Marketing Post Generator**, a built-in design tool that turns your posts into ready-to-share graphics without leaving the admin panel. It also brings Quick Edit for posts and pages, browser notifications with 20+ sounds, four new languages and a set of fixes.

### 🚀 Added

#### Marketing Post Generator

A complete graphic design studio inside RJ Nexus. Pick a post, style it, and download a polished image for social media in seconds, with no Photoshop, Canva or external service needed. It is a feature most content management systems simply do not ship with.

Open it from the **Posts** page with the new **Create Marketing Post** button.

- **Start from your own content.** Search your posts and select one. Its title, excerpt and featured image are loaded into the design automatically, so a finished graphic takes only a few clicks.
- **8 layouts.** Full image, Glass, Split, Browser, Phone, Card, Polaroid and Text only. The Browser, Phone, Card and Polaroid layouts place your screenshot or image inside a realistic frame.
- **8 quick styles.** One-click looks named Bold, Clean, Midnight, Sunset, Editorial, Neon, Ocean and Mono. Each one sets the layout, colors, font and effects together.
- **Content controls.** Add a badge (for example NEW, UPDATE or BLOG), a title, a subtitle and a call-to-action button.
- **Backgrounds.**
  - 8 ready gradient presets, a shuffle button for random color pairs, linear or radial gradients, two custom colors and an angle slider.
  - Upload your own image or screenshot, with controls for zoom, horizontal and vertical position, brightness, saturation, blur and overlay darkness.
  - **Auto colors** reads your image and builds a matching gradient from its dominant colors.
- **Typography.** Left, center or right alignment, top, middle or bottom position, three font styles (Sans, Serif, Mono), four weights, title and subtitle size, line height, uppercase mode, text shadow and text color.
- **Button styles.** Solid, Outline or Glass, with a custom button color.
- **Brand.** Your site logo and brand name are added automatically. You can show or hide them, move them to the top or bottom, resize the logo, and add your own link text.
- **Effects.** Dots, grid or line patterns with adjustable opacity, plus Orbs, Rings or Glow decorations. Add a frame with custom thickness and color, and round the corners.
- **Every social format.** 1:1 (square), 4:5 (portrait), 9:16 (story), 16:9 (wide) and 4:3 (classic), plus a **Custom** size from 200 to 4000 pixels. A **Guides** switch shows safe margins and the areas covered by the interface on stories.
- **Flexible export.**
  - PNG, JPG or WebP, with a quality slider for JPG and WebP.
  - 1x or 2x resolution, with the final pixel size shown before you download.
  - **Download** the current design, **Copy** it straight to your clipboard, or get **All sizes (ZIP)** to receive every format in a single archive.
- **Templates.** Save any design as a named template and apply or delete it later. Your settings are remembered automatically in your browser, so your last design is waiting for you next time.
- **Private and fast.** Images are generated directly in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a third-party service.
- **Fully translatable.** Every label in the tool uses the translation system.

#### Quick Edit for Posts and Pages

- Edit the **title**, the **slug** and the **current status** directly from the list, without opening the full editor.
- Change the status in one step. For example, move a published post back to **Draft** instantly.

#### Browser Notifications and Sounds

- **Browser notifications:** you are now alerted the moment a new notification arrives, even when the admin tab is in the background.
- **20+ notification sounds:** choose the sound that suits you.
- **Site cards:** cards now show an indicator when new notifications arrive.

#### RJ Apps

- Added a new **RJ Apps** section to the admin sidebar.

#### 4 New Languages

RJ Nexus now supports **48 languages**:

- `no`: Norwegian
- `fi`: Finnish
- `bg`: Bulgarian
- `sr`: Serbian

Missing translation strings were also added for **all languages**.

### ✨ Improved

- **Notifications settings:** the Notifications section in Settings is lighter and simpler to use.
- **Page edit and create screens:** cleaner, improved layout.
- **About page:** simplified.

### 🐛 Fixed

- **Layout overflow on Posts and Pages:** a long title or slug no longer breaks the page design. Long text is now handled correctly instead of overflowing the screen.
- **Missing language flags:** language flags now display correctly in the Page edit and create screens.
- **Danish flag:** the missing Danish flag was added.
- **Activity Log:** logs can be deleted again.
